Version 1.0 is the first release of this reading board. Board, Analytics, and Settings are available from the first launch. Place a book with Title, Page count, Genre isle, and Pace, then Moor this volume. Log minutes and Add to Wake. Step when Wake holds ten pages to write a TileMark and walk one tile. The first TileMark lights the lighthouse and unlocks the eight-tile ring. Wake then step and How stepping works explain the mechanic. Analytics lists TileMarks, lamps, and sittings. Settings includes Local harbor, Re-run onboarding, Reset all data, and Contact Cresset. Harbor data stays on the device.
